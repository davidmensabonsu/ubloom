1

The work & what has been delivered

The Developer has designed and built uBloom, a native iOS application, on the Client's behalf. The delivered work includes:

  • A complete iOS app (React / Capacitor) with the uBloom design system and theming.
  • Account sign-up and sign-in, including native Google and Apple sign-in.
  • A Supabase backend — database, authentication and server functions.
  • Subscriptions and the checkout experience (Stripe on web, Apple In-App Purchase via RevenueCat on iOS), including free-trial handling.
  • The "Ubi" AI companion, cycle & health tracking, journaling, routines, mood insights, and the Wander library.
  • A build & release pipeline for TestFlight and the App Store.

The build is complete. Remaining steps to publish (App Store submission and review) depend on the Client providing the required account access and information described in clause 3.

2

Fee

The total fee for the development work is £2,500.00 GBP, payable in two equal instalments as set out in clause 3. The fee covers the build described in clause 1. Ongoing revenue is dealt with separately in clause 4, and running costs in clause 5.

4

Ongoing arrangement — please choose one

In addition to the fee above, the parties agree to one of the following. The Client marks the preferred option below; the chosen option forms part of this agreement once signed.

Option A 5% revenue share The Developer receives 5% of net subscription revenue (after Apple and RevenueCat fees) for as long as the Developer maintains the app. Simple, pays as the app earns, and requires no company to be formed.
Option B 5% equity The Developer receives a 5% ownership stake in the uBloom business. This takes effect once the Client incorporates a company and both parties sign a short shareholders' agreement.

Mark one box above (Option A or Option B) to record the chosen arrangement.

5

Running costs

From launch, the Client is responsible for all ongoing running costs of the app, including:

  • Lovable subscription — £22 / month.
  • Supabase — backend & hosting, once past the free tier.
  • RevenueCat — free up to its revenue threshold, then its published fee.
  • Ubi AI usage — the per-message AI / API cost, which scales with the number of users.
  • Any other third-party service the app depends on.
6

Intellectual property

On receipt of the full £2,500.00 fee, the Developer assigns to the Client all intellectual property rights in the uBloom application created under this agreement. Until the fee is paid in full, the Developer retains ownership of the work.

The Developer may reference the project and display non-confidential screenshots in a portfolio or as a work sample.

7

Maintenance & support

Where Option A (revenue share) is chosen, the Developer will provide reasonable ongoing maintenance — bug fixes, keeping the app working through iOS and Apple policy changes, and small updates — for as long as the revenue share continues.

Larger new features or redesigns are outside this agreement and will be quoted separately. Where Option B (equity) is chosen, the parties will set out maintenance expectations in the shareholders' agreement.

8

General

This agreement is governed by the laws of England & Wales. It sets out the whole agreement between the parties on this work and replaces any earlier discussions. Any change must be agreed by both parties in writing.
